What is a Passkey?

Say goodbye to complicated passwords. A Passkey is a modern, secure way to sign in using the device you already have in your hand.

How it works:

  • It uses your device's security (Face ID, Touch ID, or Windows Hello).
  • It creates a unique cryptographic key pair just for this site.
  • Your private key never leaves your device, making it phishing-resistant.
